Adds the Slime Sling and Slime Boots from [Tinker's Construct](https://modrinth.com/mod/tinkers-construct)

## Slime Boots
Lets you bounce on any surface and prevents fall damage
![Slime Boots Crafting Recipe](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/38509f7799bb60d9f166d408d08d20f322d90989.png)
## Slime Sling
Let's you launch yourself by charging it while looking away from a block, you get launched further if you perfectly release in 1 second, indicated by a sound
![Slime Sling Crafting Recipe](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/56a6e2e84188a6211561b409d6a973b20e0619f7.png)

The Datapack and Resourcepack are the same file, that means you have to put it both in the resourcepacks and datapacks folder UNLESS you installed it as a mod(.jar file)

Uses the [Player Motion](https://modrinth.com/datapack/player_motion) Library for the Slime Sling (is not required to be installed since it's embedded into the datapack)
